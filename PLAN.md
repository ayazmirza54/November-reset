## Scoring
- 1 point per habit per day → **390 points** max.
- **Win the challenge at 80% = 312 points.**
- **Strong day** = 10+ habits. **Perfect day** = 13/13.
- Rule of the month: **never miss twice in a row.**

## Suggested workout split (4–5×/week)
- **Mon** – Upper body · **Tue** – Lower body · **Wed** – Rest / mobility walk
- **Thu** – Full body · **Fri** – Cardio / sport · **Sat** – Optional 5th session · **Sun** – Rest

## Weekly check-in (every Sunday, 10 min)
1. Open **Month → Weekly review**: points, strong days, workouts.
2. Which habit had the lowest consistency (**Stats** tab)? Fix one friction point for it.
3. Plan next week's workouts and the skill topics you'll cover.

## Setup before you start
- Clear junk food from home; stock fruit, nuts, eggs, curd.
- Put a physical alarm clock in the bedroom and charge the phone outside it.
- Pick your one skill (default in app: Terraform / AWS — editable in **Plan → Settings**).
- Choose the book(s) you'll read.
- Install the app and allow notifications for the 7:00 AM / 9:30 PM reminders.
